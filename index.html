Research Publication

A peer-reviewed transient dynamic study of motorcycle chassis frames under real-world impact loading conditions.

Transient Dynamic Analysis of a Motorcycle Frame under Real-World Impact Loading Conditions

Amol Asalekar · Neil Desai · Ruchika Navrange · Yash Sonawane
Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, MIT Academy of Engineering, Pune

Motorcycle chassis frames undergo severe transient loading during road impact events that generates peak dynamic stresses substantially exceeding static equivalents. This study develops a validated 3D finite element model of a perimeter-type chassis and performs transient structural analysis under three standardised impact load cases — frontal collision (50 km/h), rear impact (30 km/h), and pothole traversal (40 km/h) — comparing AISI 4130 steel and AA6061-T6 aluminium alloy frames. Dynamic Amplification Factors of 1.72–2.31 were quantified, demonstrating that static FEA alone is structurally non-conservative by 72–131%.
